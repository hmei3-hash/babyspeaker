Babies talk all day. Just not in words.

A pointed finger, a change in pitch, a face that crumples a second before the cry. The signals are there, and they are easy to miss. Parents end up either watching constantly and wearing out, or looking away and wondering what they missed.

BabySpeaker is a third option. Hand the watching to a model, keep your phone locked, and look up when your baby is actually reaching out to you.

What works today

Three states. One glance. No app to babysit.

During together time, every moment lands in one of three states on your lock screen. You never have to open the app.

All calm

Playing, content, asleep. Nothing needs you, so nothing interrupts you.

Needs your attention

Your baby is reaching out: pointing at a plane, calling for you. One notification, in their voice: "Mom, look!"

A bit fussy

Restless, not crying. A quiet heads-up when the evidence is weaker, so you decide.

How it decides

Built on the Perceptron Mk1.5 vision and audio model, with our own fusion layer on top. This part runs today.

  1. Watch and listen in 10-second windowsPicture and sound are read separately for cues: pointing, gaze, limb movement, cooing, fussing, crying, background TV.
  2. Check whether sound and picture agreeAgree, compatible, conflicting, or only one side usable. Disagreement is treated as a signal, not averaged away.
  3. Interrupt only on real evidenceAn alert needs about 20 seconds of agreeing evidence. Each alert shows the clip and the cues behind it, and "can't tell" is an allowed answer.
Early results

The right state on 10 of 12 test clips, up from 7.

We ran Perceptron Mk1.5 on 12 short infant clips and compared one direct prompt ("What does the baby need?") with two versions of our harness. Version 2 reads picture and sound in separate passes so one signal can't be counted twice.

Where we're going

From reading cues to helping every serve get a return.

Step one was making cue detection accurate. Everything next builds on it.

NEXT

Cues into words

Map what we detect, like pointing, looking up and excited babble, to a short line in your baby's voice: "Look! Plane!"

LATER

Serve-and-return

Notice when your baby "serves" with a point, a babble or a reach, and gently nudge you to return it. See those exchanges grow over time.

LATER

Co-viewing

Understand what your baby is looking at in a book or on a shared screen, and suggest something to talk about together.

LATER

Live on the lock screen

A Live Activity and Dynamic Island view, so the current state is one glance away without a single notification.

LATER

A private highlights reel

First times and best days, saved automatically and kept on your phone.

ONGOING

Measured, not guessed

A labeled evaluation set to measure how often each state is right, and to tune how quickly we alert.
